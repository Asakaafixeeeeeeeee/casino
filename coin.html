    How to play

    1. While the bar runs down ("Waiting for Next Round"), set your bet and tap HEADS (the crown) or TAILS (the four suits). Tap your pick again to take the bet back before the flip.
    2. When the bar empties, bets lock and the coin is flipped.
    3. If it lands on your side you win 2× your bet.
    4. Now and then the coin lands standing on its edge — then every bet loses.

    Fairness: each flip is drawn at random when the bets lock: heads 48.5%, tails 48.5%, edge 3%. A call wins 48.5% of the time at 2×, so the game returns 97% on average. Each flip is independent — the history doesn't change the next one.
