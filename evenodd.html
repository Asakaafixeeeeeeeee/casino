How to play

  1. Set your bet with the slider or a chip.
  2. Tap EVEN or ODD: three dice are rolled and added up.
  3. If the total matches your call you win 2× your bet.
  4. If all three dice show the same number (a triple), every bet loses — even if the total matched.
  5. Rebet plays the same call and bet again; New Round lets you change them.

Fairness: each die is a fair, independent 1–6, rolled the moment you call. Of the 216 equally likely rolls, 105 win for EVEN and 105 win for ODD (the 6 triples lose), so a call wins 48.6% of the time and the game returns 97.2% on average.
