How to play

  1. Place one or two bets during the 5-second countdown.
  2. The plane takes off and the multiplier climbs from 1.00x.
  3. Cash out before the plane flies away to win your bet × the multiplier.
  4. If the plane flies away first, that bet is lost.

Auto: switch a panel to Auto to bet every round and cash out at a multiplier you choose.

Fairness: the chance of reaching any multiplier m is RTP ÷ m, so every strategy returns on average over many rounds. Playing for real money, our server runs your rounds: it fixes where each one ends before betting closes, tells no one until it's over, and judges a cash-out at the moment it arrives.
