How to play & the odds

Place a stake while the game says Place your bet (8 seconds between rounds). When the jet takes off the multiplier climbs from 1.00x by 0.001 every 1/60 of a second (0.06x a second). Press Cash Out before it flies away to win stake × the multiplier at that moment. If it flies away first, the stake is lost.

Odds. The crash point is set before each round and isn't shown until the jet flies away. The chance the jet gets past x is 98% ÷ x, so about 3 rounds in 100 end at take-off and every way of cashing out returns at most 98% of stakes on average. The jet never goes past 10.70x: a bet still flying when it gets there is paid 10.70x.

With free credits (GHS 3,000 each session) your browser draws each round. With real money (sign in and open Jet from the lobby) our server draws it with the same odds, takes your bets from your wallet and judges each cash-out the moment it arrives.
