How to play

  1. Make a bet — or two at once — before take-off. A bet made while the plane is flying waits for the next round.
  2. Watch the plane climb: the multiplier starts at 1.00x and keeps rising.
  3. Cash out before the plane flies away to win your bet × the multiplier. If it flies away first, the bet is lost.

Auto: on a panel's Auto tab you can bet every round, and cash out by itself at a multiplier you choose.

One round for everyone: every player sees the same plane. The bets list shows real players' bets in this round; names are masked.

Game rules

  • A round takes bets for 8 seconds after the last plane flew away. Then the plane takes off and the multiplier climbs: e0.09 × seconds, so 2x after about 8 seconds, 10x after about 26.
  • Each round's crash point is set before take-off and told to no one until the plane has flown away.
  • The chance the plane reaches any multiplier m is 97% ÷ m. About 4 rounds in 100 fly away at 1.00x. Whichever way you play, bets return 97% on average over many rounds (RTP 97%).
  • A cash-out pays your bet × the multiplier at the moment our server receives it, rounded down to the cent. An auto cash-out pays exactly its multiplier.
  • A bet can win at most GHS 250,000: it cashes itself out when it gets there. The highest round is 10,000x.
  • If your connection drops, your bets still count: auto cash-outs still pay, and a bet nobody cashed out loses when the plane flies away.
  • Playing with free credits uses the same rounds and odds; credits are not money and can't be withdrawn.

Provably fair

Before each round takes off we publish a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of a secret seed. After the plane flies away we show the seed itself. The crash point comes from the seed, so you can check that it was fixed before the round and not changed after.

Next round's fingerprint…

To check a round, tap its multiplier in the strip above the plane. Your browser works out the crash point from the seed: HMAC-SHA256 with the seed as key over “sky-rush:” and the round number; the first 13 hex digits, divided by 252, give u; the crash point is ⌊97 ÷ (1 − u)⌋ ÷ 100, at least 1.00x.
