How to play

  1. Choose your bet with the slider or a chip.
  2. Tap UP or DOWN — the bottle spins.
  3. If the neck stops in your half, you win 2× your bet.
  4. If it stops on the MIDDLE line (the thin red wedges), every bet loses.

Fairness: the bottle stops at a random angle. UP and DOWN each cover 48% of the circle and the MIDDLE wedges 4%, so the game returns 96% on average.
