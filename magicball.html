How to play

  1. Pick a chip, then tap SPOT 1, 2 or 3 to put it there — tap again to add more. You can cover up to two spots.
  2. Press Place Bet: the three cups lift together to show where the ball is.
  3. If the ball is under one of your spots, that spot pays 2.9× its chips. The bin clears your chips.
  4. Shuffle Again (or Reshuffle) brings the cups down and shuffles for the next round. Your chips stay where they are.

Fairness: the ball's spot is drawn at random when you place your bet — one time in three for each spot. The shuffle is for show, so following the cups doesn't help, and nothing is steered. A spot pays 2.9× at odds of 1 in 3, so the game returns 96.7% on average, whether you cover one spot or two. A spot takes at most GHS 10,000.
