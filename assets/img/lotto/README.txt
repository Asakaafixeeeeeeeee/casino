LOTTO PHOTOGRAPHS
=================

The Lotto page looks for these files and shows a plain black/red/yellow panel until they exist:

  hero-celebration.jpg   main banner (landscape, about 1600x900)
  promo-winners.jpg      "Daily draws" banner (about 1200x700)
  promo-payout.jpg       "Paid to your balance" banner (about 1200x700)

Use only photographs of real people that you took yourself or have a licence for
(a paid stock library, or a free licence that allows commercial use and does not require
attribution you aren't giving). Keep the licence receipt. Do not use AI-generated people,
and do not copy images from other lotto sites.
Save as JPG, under ~300 KB each, with the subject away from the bottom-left (text sits there).
