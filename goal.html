How to play

  1. Pick a field size and your bet, then press BET — or just tap a tile in the first column, which places your bet and plays it.
  2. Start at the left: tap one tile in the glowing column. A ball moves you up the pitch to the next column and raises the multiplier; a bomb ends the round and the bet is lost.
  3. Press CASH OUT any time after your first ball. Clearing the last column — a GOAL — cashes out by itself at the top multiplier.
  4. RANDOM taps a random tile in the column. With Auto Game on, choose one tile in as many columns as you like, press BET, and they're opened in order, cashing out once they're all balls.

Fairness: each column hides exactly one bomb, placed at random when you press BET; a tile only shows what is already under it. Each multiplier is 97% divided by the chance of getting that far, rounded down to 0.01 — so whenever you cash out the game returns at most 97% on average. The most one round can pay is GHS 100,000.
