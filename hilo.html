    How to play & odds

    1. A card is dealt face up. Call whether the next card will be HIGHER or LOWER. Aces are high; a card of the same rank loses.
    2. Choose your bet, tap a call and confirm. The next card turns over.
    3. A right call pays the multiplier shown on the button: 97% ÷ the chance of that call, rounded down to the cent. A sure thing pays little; a long shot pays a lot.
    4. Play Next Hand moves the pair to the hands column and deals a fresh card. New Round clears the column.

    Fairness: the next card is one of the other 51 cards of a full deck, each equally likely — for real money it's drawn and paid by our server, which prices the call from the card showing. The return of each call is its multiplier × its chance; every call returns between 96.47% and 96.94%, and the average over every call is 96.8%.
