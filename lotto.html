Fair draws

Numbers locked before betting

Daily and standard draws use numbers generated by the server from a secret seed. For named draws, an admin may choose five numbers before ticket sales open; their salted fingerprint is committed before sales and the choice cannot be changed. Each draw's fingerprint is shown while you play, and its seed is published with the result so the commitment can be checked.

Account security

Your account and your money, protected

Passwords never stored as text

Your password is kept only as a salted scrypt hash, so it can't be read back by anyone.

Sign-ins that can't be copied

Each log-in gets a random session token. We store only its fingerprint, so a copy of our database can't be used to sign in.

Password guessing is blocked

Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a while, and sign-in attempts from each address are limited.

Winnings go only to you

Withdrawals are paid only to the mobile money number you registered with.

Your PIN and card stay with you

You approve mobile money payments with your PIN on your own phone, and card payments on TheTeller's secure page. We never see your PIN or card number.

You stay in control

Set deposit limits or take a break from your account whenever you want, from My account.
