Provably fair · how it works

Everyone connected plays the same board for 3 minutes. At the start of a round the server shows the SHA-256 hash of a secret seed: –. The 6 mines are worked out from that seed. When the round ends, the seed and the mines are published, so you can check the hash matches and re-derive the mines (verify the last round).

Choose 1–19 tiles and press BET to lock them in — one bet per round, and you can cancel it until the last 10 seconds. Nothing is revealed until the clock hits 0:00; then the board turns over for everyone. If all your tiles are stars you win your bet × the multiplier for that many tiles; if any is a mine, the bet is lost. The multiplier for k tiles is 96.5% ÷ the chance that k tiles are all safe (1 tile 1.27x, 2 tiles 1.69x, 3 tiles 2.29x …), so every bet returns 96.5% on average. Auto Game places the same tiles and stake again every round.

Playing for real money (open Live Mines from the RendoWin lobby while signed in), your bet comes from your RendoWin wallet: the wallet service draws each round's seed, keeps it secret until 0:00, and pays wins straight into your wallet — even if you close this page before the round ends.
