How to play

  1. Set your bet and pick a difficulty, then press Bet: the hen steps into the first lane.
  2. Each lane she gets through is marked by a blue barricade and raises the multiplier. Press Go (or tap the glowing manhole) to step into the next lane.
  3. Press Cash out any time after the first lane to take your bet × the current multiplier. If a car hits her, the bet is lost. Reaching the far side cashes out by itself.

Fairness: how far the hen gets is drawn once, at random, the moment you press Bet; the road then just plays it out and nothing is steered. The chance of getting through a lane and every lane before it is 97% divided by that lane's multiplier, so whenever you cash out the game returns 97% on average. The most one round can pay is GHS 100,000 — reaching it cashes out by itself, and on high-stake bets far up the road the cap lowers the average return.
