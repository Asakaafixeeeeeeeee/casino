How to play

  1. Set your bet with − and +, then press Play: the biplane takes off from the first carrier.
  2. The route crosses up to 6 carriers, with 2 or 3 pickups or bombs between one and the next. +1, +2, +5, +10 add to the multiplier and lift the plane, ×2, ×3, ×4, ×5 multiply it; a bomb on its line halves it (−50%, rounded down to the cent) and knocks the plane down.
  3. The flight can end anywhere. On each stretch the plane may go down in the sea before the next carrier — the bet is lost. Over each carrier it reaches, it either lands by itself — and you win your bet × the multiplier at that moment (at most ×250) — or flies on. Past the last carrier the fuel runs out.
  4. The menu's Speed setting only changes how fast the flight is shown.

Fairness: the whole flight — its events, how many carriers it passes and where it lands — is drawn at random the moment you press Play; the flight then just plays it out. Each event: . On each stretch the plane goes down with a 30% chance, and over each carrier it reaches it lands with a 19.27% chance — so 30.0% of flights land, and because the multiplier keeps growing along the route, that makes the game return exactly 97% on average. Only what lies on the plane's line counts — the other numbers and bombs floating in the sky are scenery and never touch it.
