How to play

  1. Choose how many mines (1–24) are hidden on the 5×5 board, and your bet.
  2. Press BET. Tap a tile: a star keeps you in the round, a mine ends it and the bet is lost.
  3. Every star raises your payout. Next shows the multiplier you'd reach with one more star — press CASH OUT any time to take it.
  4. RANDOM opens a random tile. With Auto Game on, tap the tiles you want, press BET, and they open one by one — cashing out once they're all safe.

Fairness: the mines are placed at random when you press BET, and a tile only shows what's already under it. Each multiplier is 96.5% divided by the chance of finding that many stars in a row (rounded to 0.01), so the game returns about 96.5% on average whatever you do. The most one round can pay is GHS 100,000, so on very high-risk, high-stake bets the cap lowers the average return.
