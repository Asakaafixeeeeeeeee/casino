      How to play

      1. Pick a chip (0.1, 1, 10, 20 or 100) and tap the felt to place it. Tap again to add more. Clear takes all chips off; RESET takes back the last one.
      2. Press SPIN. The ball lands in one of 13 pockets, 0 to 12.
      3. Pays: a single number (including 0) 11:1 · a row C1/C2/C3 (4 numbers) 2:1 · RED, BLACK, ODD, EVEN, 1-6, 7-12 (6 numbers each) 1:1. 0 is neither red nor black, odd nor even.
      4. AUTO PLAY repeats your last bet every round until you tap it again (at most 50 rounds).

      Fairness: the pocket is drawn at random from the 13, each equally likely, the moment you press SPIN; the wheel then plays out a spin that ends there. Every bet returns 12/13 of what's staked on average — 92.3%, the usual return of 13-pocket mini roulette.
