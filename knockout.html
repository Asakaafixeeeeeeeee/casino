How to play

  1. While the bell is about to ring ("Waiting for Next Round"), set an amount on one or both panels and press BET. Press CANCEL to take a bet back before the round starts.
  2. Classic: when the fight starts the multiplier climbs from 1.00x. Press CASH OUT any time to win your bet × the multiplier. At the knockout, every bet not cashed out is lost. Auto can place your bet every round and cash out at a multiplier you choose.
  3. ColorGo: call the colour the round ends in — Blue (knockout below 2x), Purple (2x – 9.99x) or Red (10x or more). Pays: . Red pays the moment the fight reaches 10x.

Fairness: the knockout point is drawn at random when the round starts; the chance of the fight lasting to any multiplier m is RTP ÷ m, so every Classic strategy returns on average. Each ColorGo colour pays the RTP divided by its chance, rounded down, so it returns at most the same. Playing for real money, our server runs your rounds: it fixes where each one ends before betting closes, tells no one until it's over, and judges a cash-out at the moment it arrives.
