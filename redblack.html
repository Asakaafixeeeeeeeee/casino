    How to play

    1. Choose your bet with the slider or a chip.
    2. Tap RED (hearts and diamonds) or BLACK (spades and clubs) — the top card of the deck is turned over.
    3. If its colour matches your call, you win 2× your bet.
    4. If a Joker turns up, every call loses.
    5. With credits, cards aren't put back: the deck runs down through the round and the history shows every card drawn. New Round shuffles a full deck.
    6. With real money, every hand is dealt by our server from a freshly shuffled full deck of 54, so every call has the same 96.3% return.

    Fairness: each round's deck is shuffled at random, then dealt in order — no card is ever picked to suit a call. A full deck holds 26 red cards, 26 black and 2 Jokers, so a call on a fresh deck wins 26 times in 54: a return of 96.3%. As the deck runs down, the odds move with what's left.
